# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard

> An interactive **Excel Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using 197K+ Swiggy food delivery records to analyze sales performance, customer ratings, food preferences, order trends, and geographical performance.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a large Swiggy food delivery dataset containing **197K+ records** to understand sales performance and customer behavior across different dimensions.

The interactive Excel dashboard transforms raw transactional data into business insights using:

* KPI cards
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* Interactive filters
* Data visualization

The project focuses on understanding **sales trends, customer preferences, ratings, food categories, and regional performance**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer key business questions:

* How are sales changing over time?
* Which cities and states generate the highest sales?
* Which food categories are most preferred?
* How do customer ratings vary?
* What are the key order and sales trends?
* Which regions demonstrate stronger business performance?

---

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important business metrics such as:

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Orders**
* **Average Order Value**
* **Average Customer Rating**
* **Total Ratings**
* **Sales by Region**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall business performance.

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

### 👥 Customer Analysis

### 🌍 Geographic Analysis

Analyze sales performance across:

Analyze customer ratings to understand overall satisfaction patterns and their relationship with sales performance.
